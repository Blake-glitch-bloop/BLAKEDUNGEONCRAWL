YOUR FIELD GUIDE

A hero in two thumbs.

Drag the joystick at the bottom left to move. Hold the big sword button at the bottom right to attack nearby enemies.

Clear the little slimes, then defeat the floor’s guardian. When the glowing stairway opens, walk into it to descend. Each floor restores two hearts.

The guardian glows red before charging. Move out of its path, then get close and strike!

On a keyboard: WASD / arrows to move, Space to attack, Esc / P to pause. Your best floor is saved on this device. Five floors complete the adventure.
